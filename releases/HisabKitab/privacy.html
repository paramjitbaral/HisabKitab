HisabKitab Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026
Developer: Paramjit Baral
Contact: aapnastoreofficial@gmail.com

Offline records

HisabKitab is an offline personal ledger. It does not require an account, phone number, OTP or internet connection. Your borrower names, loan amounts, due dates, notes, repayment status, optional profile name and selected photo are stored or accessed locally on your device. The app does not transmit these records to Paramjit Baral or an app-operated server.

No advertising or tracking

The Android release contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs and does not request internet, contacts, SMS, location or broad photo-library permissions.

Photos and files you choose

You can choose an optional profile image using Android's file picker. Access is limited to the selected file. CSV exports and JSON backups are written only to a location you select. A JSON backup contains loan records, not your profile photo. If you choose a cloud file provider, that provider processes the selected file according to its own privacy policy; HisabKitab does not operate that service. Backup and CSV files are not encrypted by HisabKitab. Keep them private.

Storage and security

Loan records are stored in the app's private Android storage. Protect your phone with a screen lock. HisabKitab does not provide a separate app PIN or database encryption. Automatic Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup are disabled. Create a JSON backup before uninstalling, clearing app storage or changing phones.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you remove them. Profile → Your Data → Delete all app data removes the loans and profile stored by this installation after a typed confirmation. You can remove the profile image in Personal Information. Uninstalling or clearing Android app storage also removes app-private records. Exported files and original images outside the app remain wherever you saved them; delete those separately. There is no remote account to delete.

Support

If you email aapnastoreofficial@gmail.com, Paramjit Baral receives the message and any information you choose to include through the email provider. Support messages are used to respond and resolve the request. Do not send sensitive loan records unless needed. You may request deletion of support correspondence by email, subject to any applicable legal retention obligations. Email-provider processing follows that provider's policy.

Children

HisabKitab is a general personal record-keeping tool and is not designed or marketed specifically to children. No data is automatically collected from any user through the app. The developer may receive information voluntarily submitted in a support email.

Changes

This policy will be updated when the app's data practices change. The effective date identifies the current policy. Questions can be sent to the contact above.
